Block Puzzle – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Block Puzzle game for Android (package com.ngkg.blockpuzzle) collects and why. The game is made by Kunal Gandhi ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, write to kunalcoolness88@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your phone.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and uses Google Firebase to count how the game is played and to report crashes.
  • In the EU, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before ads are personalised or play statistics are collected, and you can change your answer at any time.
  • We do not sell your information.

Information stored on your phone

Your finished levels, hints, and settings (sound, vibration, appearance) are saved on your device only. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and paid for by ads served by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google collects information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system and language, and how you interact with ads.

In the EU, the UK and Switzerland, a consent form from Google is shown when the game first starts. If you don't consent, only non-personalised ads (based on context, not on your interests) are shown where permitted. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Play statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

With your consent where the law requires it, the game records how it is played, for example which levels are started and finished, how many moves a level took, and when hints are used. Firebase also records basic information such as the device model, operating system version, app version, approximate country (from the IP address) and an app-instance identifier. We use this only to improve the game, for example to find levels that are too hard. It is not used for advertising and not linked to the advertising ID.

Firebase keeps this data for 2 months.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the game crashes, a crash report is sent so we can fix the problem. It contains technical details: what went wrong in the code, the level being played, the device model, operating system version, app version, and an installation identifier. It contains nothing about you personally. Crash reports are kept for 90 days. We process them on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the game working.

Your choices and rights

  • Change your consent: in the EU, the UK and Switzerland, open the game's settings and tap Privacy choices.
  • Stop all collection: uninstall the game.
  • Access or deletion: because we hold no names or emails, we usually can't link data to you. If you'd like us to try, or have any other request, email us and we'll reply within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Where data is processed

Google may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in Google's privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.

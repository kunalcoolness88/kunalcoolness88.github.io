Ghostdoku Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Ghostdoku app ("the app", "we") handles, and why. Ghostdoku is a puzzle game for Android.

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your phone. We do not have a server.
  • The app sends anonymous gameplay statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase, so we can see which levels are too hard and fix bugs. These are not linked to your name or any account.
  • The app shows ads. The ad companies listed below collect some device information to show and measure those ads.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the puzzles you have solved, your scores, whether you have seen the tutorial, and your sound and vibration settings. This is stored locally on your device only. Your saved progress itself is never uploaded, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Gameplay statistics and crash reports

The app uses Google Firebase, a service provided by Google, for two things:

  • Firebase Analytics records how the game is played: when a level is started and finished, its number, size and difficulty, how long it took, whether you ran out of mistakes, and when you watch an ad for a hint or to continue.
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends a report when the app crashes or hits a serious error, including the error details and the state of the app at that moment.

Along with this, Firebase collects device information (such as model, operating system version, language and approximate location derived from your IP address), an app instance identifier that is random and specific to this installation, and your device's advertising ID.

We use this only to understand how the game is played, balance the puzzles and fix crashes. We see it as totals and anonymous reports, not tied to your name, email or any account, and we do not sell it. Google handles this data under its own privacy policy, linked above, and keeps it only for a limited time.

Advertising

The app is free and supported by ads, served by Google AdMob. Some ads may be delivered by Meta Audience Network through AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, these companies may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived;
  • device information, such as model, operating system version and language;
  • how you interact with ads (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped);
  • diagnostic information, such as crashes or ad-loading problems.

This information is collected and handled by those companies under their own privacy policies, linked above. We do not receive it in a form that identifies you. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): if you are in one of these regions, the app asks for your consent before ads are personalised, using Google's consent tool. You can refuse, and the app still works. You can change your choice at any time: tap the gear on the home screen, then Privacy options.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device). Deleting it stops it from being used for personalised ads.
  • Deleting your data: uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone. It also stops any further gameplay statistics and crash reports. Reports already sent are not tied to your identity, so we cannot pick out yours; they are deleted automatically when Google's retention period ends.

Children

Ghostdoku is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it where we can.

Security

Ad data, gameplay statistics and crash reports are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) by the ad providers' and Google Firebase's software.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

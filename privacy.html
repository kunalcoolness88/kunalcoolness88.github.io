Ghostdoku Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Ghostdoku app ("the app", "we") handles, and why. Ghostdoku is a puzzle game for Android.

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your phone. We do not have a server, and we never receive them.
  • The app shows ads. The ad companies listed below collect some device information to show and measure those ads.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the puzzles you have solved, your scores, whether you have seen the tutorial, and your sound and vibration settings. This is stored locally on your device only. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising

The app is free and supported by ads, served by Google AdMob. Some ads may be delivered by Meta Audience Network through AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, these companies may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived;
  • device information, such as model, operating system version and language;
  • how you interact with ads (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped);
  • diagnostic information, such as crashes or ad-loading problems.

This information is collected and handled by those companies under their own privacy policies, linked above. We do not receive it in a form that identifies you. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): if you are in one of these regions, the app asks for your consent before ads are personalised, using Google's consent tool. You can refuse, and the app still works.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device). Deleting it stops it from being used for personalised ads.
  • Deleting your data: uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone.

Children

Ghostdoku is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it where we can.

Security

Ad data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) by the ad providers' software.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
